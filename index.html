About Me

I’m a UX/UI designer with a Master’s in User Experience Research & Design from the University of Michigan, grounded in a background in cognitive science and programming — I approach design problems with both a human-behavior lens and a logical, systems-level understanding of how they’re built. I most recently lead a UI Design team improving the interface of 1Cademy, a collaborative learning platform. Outside of design, I create and perform music, and play tennis, volleyball, and explore photography.

My Design Philosophy

Design means a lot of different things to me. While I find it to be a fun and creative outlet, I also believe it comes with responsibility. Design can only be as positively influential as the people behind it. The technology we create is neither inherently good, nor inherently bad — it merely reflects who we are. Without mindfulness, this can have negative impacts; if we are careful and intentional, I believe it can reflect who we hope to be. I strongly believe it is the job of any designer to consider the ethical and moral implications of their design for users, and that all design should strive to achieve inclusivity and accessibility for all users — prioritizing minorities and marginalized groups from the beginning of the design process. I hope to find like-minded designers who approach their work with similar values and have a respect and appreciation for the power design can possess.

My Background

I recently completed my Master’s in “User Experience Research & Design” at the University of Michigan in May of 2022.

Prior to my master’s, I received dual degrees in both UX Design and Cognitive Science, along with a minor in music. I have a background in programming and logical reasoning, as understood from both a mathematical and human cognitive perspective — giving me a strong understanding of people and how they make decisions, as well as an exceptional attention to detail. Currently, I’m the Project Leader of a UI Design Community working to improve the user interface of an online collaborative learning platform called 1Cademy. My education and life experience have given me a genuine appreciation for the importance of designing with people in mind.
