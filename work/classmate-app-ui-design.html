Classmate App Design

Creative UI Solution for Facilitating School-Related Social Groups

My Roles
UX Researcher, UI Designer
Collaborators
Masum, Caroline, Kelley
Timeline
Sept 2020 – Dec 2020

Project Overview

My team was asked to design an interactive interface for a target population that did not consist primarily of college students, and our design needed to meaningfully incorporate some kind of social element.

Our solution was “Classmate,” an app designed specifically for high school students, who most likely have prior experience with mobile technology and social media apps. We chose to design an application that could bridge the gap between a social media platform and an online learning platform — a student-run space where high-schoolers sign up under their specific school and can communicate with all other students at their school, as well as view extracurricular activities and study groups.

Process

Sketches & Brainstorming — goal: come up with as many unique ideas as possible regardless of sensibility or feasibility. I didn’t stop to evaluate how well my ideas solved the problem, I simply noted and sketched all my ideas. The team then decided to go with a mobile app design solution.

Paper Prototype — goal: demonstrate the main parts of our app design. My team discussed key features, then each created paper prototypes for different parts of the app to quickly demonstrate those ideas and conduct some lightweight user testing.

Initial Wireframes — goal: map out the rough layout and content of each page. Each of us took a section of the app and built wireframes, this time in Figma.

Hi-fidelity Wireframes — goal: further map out specific elements and UI components each page needs. We each took a section again and further developed the content and layout using Whimsical.

Style Guide — goal: create a color palette and components for our app. We discussed text font, primary and secondary colors, and ultimately decided on one primary color (shades of pink) plus various shades of gray as accents.

Final Prototype — we divided the work by each taking a section of the app flow and designing that set of screens, then reviewed each other’s work and tested the prototype as a whole.
