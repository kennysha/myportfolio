EAS Lilypad App

Creative UI Solution for Displaying Autistic Patients’ Profile Data to Therapists

My Role
UI Designer
Timeline
May 2020 – Sept 2020

Missing asset

No images were recoverable for this project — the hi-fi prototype lived only in a Figma file.

I worked with a small team of UX designers and researchers to develop an app that helps therapists organize and track data on their autistic patients.

Process

I started with an initial brainstorm of functionalities of the Client Profile portion of the app. Created initial design sketches, turned this into a mockup using Figma, then developed a prototype. Preference-tested my design against a different one with users, and moved forward with the design I created, incorporating certain aspects from the other design and using feedback from the user to evaluate the necessity of certain elements. Slightly re-designed the page after officially deciding which functionalities to keep, then wrote the final documentation for the application’s functionalities and content. I received a style guide for the entire application from my team leader and used this to create a high-fidelity working prototype of the Client Profile page.

Outcome &amp; Takeaways

This project taught me about the UX design process and how to go from initial sketches to full hi-fidelity prototypes using Figma. I took responsibility for my portion of the application, the Client Profile, and took the lead on its development, working with a team of 5 other colleagues and a team leader who offered guidance and support but left a lot of decisions on design up to me. I enjoyed having the autonomy to create something helpful for the user on my own, as well as coming together as a group to review other people’s work on other portions of the application.
