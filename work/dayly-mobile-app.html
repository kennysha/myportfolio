Dayly Mobile App

Mobile App Prototype for Student Task Management and Planning

Collaborators
Emily Hobrla, Taylor Pessetti, and Judy Ng
My Role
UX Designer
Timeline
Jan 2022 – April 2022

Problem Space

In the ideation phase of this project, our team discussed an interest in solving a common issue we each experience, where college students find it difficult to track and manage school assignments and their due dates, with daily personal tasks.

During the competitive analysis phase, our team found there appeared to be no single app that allows students to track all their daily tasks. Neither Canvas nor Google Calendar keep track of school assignments and daily tasks simultaneously or automatically — students have to manually input everything: school assignments, daily events, reminders and tasks.

Based on this, our team wanted to explore a way to either optimize current available platforms, or create a new interface to integrate the different sources of tasks all in one place.
