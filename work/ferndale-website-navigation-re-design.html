Ferndale City Website Navigation Re-design

Information Architecture Re-design for the City of Ferndale’s Website

My Roles
UX Researcher & Strategist, UI Designer
Collaborators
Marivi, Safa, Grace Song
Timeline
Sept 2021 – Dec 2021

Project Impact

We received very positive feedback from our client within the communications department, as well as people in the Ferndale community. We were informed that the city still has another year left on the contract for their current website, so they won’t be able to implement our new design until 2023, but because of our team’s evaluation of the site, they are seriously considering re-designing and re-structuring their website when the contract is up — they feel they have a better sense of what needs improving, and will likely use our designs to inform future changes.
