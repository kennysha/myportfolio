Modern Victorian Mood Board

For this project my goal was to capture the essence of the Victorian era, Baroque, style, yet add my own modern interpretation with some lighter colors.

Modern Victorian Mood Board
with Milanote
I created a lighter, brighter take on the Victorian style. 
True to the style, I included a vintage gallery wall, both wood flooring and intricate tile flooring, as well as some intricately patterned wallpaper and wall trim/moldings, but I kept the colors less dark and intense overall. I included vintage/Victorian style furniture like the floor lamp and the sofas and chaise, which have curved backs and ornate details. 
The main color pallet includes white, medium to light brown, tan, as well as purples, pinks, and greens, with a lot of gold accents.
